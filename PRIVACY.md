# Mandi Bhav privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Mandi Bhav is an Android app that shows agricultural market (APMC mandi) prices. It is the
reference app for RizTech Academy's Android Development with Kotlin course. This policy says what
the app does with information about you. The short version: it collects nothing and shares
nothing.

## What the app collects and shares

Nothing. Mandi Bhav has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does
not send any information about you, or about how you use it, to RizTech Academy or to anyone else.

## What the app uses on your phone

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| The internet connection | To download prices and commodity pictures | The app asks Mandi Bhav's server, a set of files on GitHub, for the same files everyone gets. It sends nothing about you. Like any website, GitHub's servers see your phone's internet (IP) address when the files are requested; Mandi Bhav does not use or keep it. GitHub's handling of it is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). |
| Approximate location, if you allow it | To sort markets by distance when you tap "Use my location" | Used once, on your phone. It is not saved and not sent anywhere. |
| Notifications, if you allow them | To tell you when a price crosses one of your alerts | Created on your phone after a price refresh. No server sends them. |
| Photos you take or choose of a sale slip | To keep the slip with that commodity and market | Copied into the app's private storage on your phone. Not uploaded. |
| Your price alerts and home market | To check your alerts and show your market first | Kept on your phone. Your home market is included in your phone's own backup if backup is turned on in Android's settings; nothing else from the app is. |

## Deleting your data

Everything Mandi Bhav keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in
Android's settings, deletes all of it.

## Children

Mandi Bhav is not directed at children.

## Changes and contact

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will change first, with a new date at the top.
